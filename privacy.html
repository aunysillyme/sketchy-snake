Privacy Policy for Sketchy Snake

Effective Date: September 2026

Sketchy Snake is an independent, single-player arcade game created by Auny (aunysillyme.dev). We believe in user privacy and straightforward software that respects players.

1. Zero Data Collection

Sketchy Snake does NOT collect, harvest, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII), device identifiers, location data, or telemetry.

2. Local Storage Only

All game progress, personal high scores, and sound volume preferences are stored solely on your local device (via browser localStorage or local app storage). This data never leaves your device and is not shared with any external servers.

3. No Advertisements & No Tracking

Sketchy Snake contains NO third-party advertisements, tracking pixels, ad networks, or analytics SDKs.

4. Audio & Haptics

All music and sound effects are generated procedurally on-device using the Web Audio API. Tactile vibration feedback uses standard local device haptics with no remote telemetry.

5. Children's Privacy

Because Sketchy Snake collects no personal information whatsoever, it complies fully with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and GDPR regulations.

6. Contact

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this policy, feel free to reach out via GitHub at github.com/aunysillyme/sketchy-snake or email aunysillyme@gmail.com.
